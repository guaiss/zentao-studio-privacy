Privacy Policy — Noted

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Noted ("the app", "we") is a notification-history app for Android published by ZenTao Studio. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Summary

Your data never leaves your phone. Noted has no internet permission, so it is technically unable to send anything anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking. Noted does not collect, transmit, share or sell personal data.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Notification access (Android "Notification access" permission, granted by you in system settings after an in-app explanation). Noted reads the notifications posted on your device — the app that posted it, title, text, chat messages and sender names, time and, if you bought Noted Pro, pictures carried by the notification — so that you can read them later, search them and see messages the sender deleted.
  • Post notifications (optional): used only for keyword alerts you set up and to warn you if Noted has stopped recording.
  • Google Play Billing: used only to check whether you bought the one-time Noted Pro unlock.

Where your data is stored

  • Only in Noted's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it.
  • Excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • You choose which apps are recorded, and you can delete all history at any time in Settings. Uninstalling Noted removes everything.
  • Free version keeps 7 days of history; Noted Pro keeps it until you delete it.

When data leaves your device — only by your action

  • Export as CSV (Pro): creates a file and opens the Android share sheet; you decide where it goes.
  • Create backup: writes a file encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256-GCM, key derived with PBKDF2) to a location you pick. Noted cannot read a backup without that password, and nobody can recover a forgotten password.

Purchases

Noted Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. Noted only learns whether the purchase exists.

Children

Noted is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
