Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-02. Publisher: Zentao Studio.

Sidepad stores notes, titles, editing position and display preferences only in the application's private local storage. It has no internet or microphone permission, analytics/advertising SDK, account, accessibility service or server upload. It does not read the content of the application beneath its overlay.

Keyboard typing and dictation are provided by the user's chosen input method. That separate app controls its microphone permissions, speech processing and any network service; its own privacy policy applies to that processing.

The overlay permission displays the user-started floating editor. Foreground-service and optional notification permissions provide visible operation and stop controls. No device identifiers, contacts, location or installed-app inventory are collected by Sidepad.

Notes are not password-encrypted and app-data cloud/device-transfer backup is excluded. Uninstalling removes local notes. A user may deliberately copy text to the Android clipboard, share it with a selected app, or export notes/recovery files to a chosen location. Those copies leave app-private storage and are unencrypted; the selected destination's policies apply. Import only reads a user-selected file.

There is no data collected or shared with the publisher's servers. Delete a note in Notes to remove it locally; uninstall to remove all private app data. Copies previously exported/shared must be managed separately at their destinations.

Sidepad does not provide a cloud account or remote data-deletion service.
