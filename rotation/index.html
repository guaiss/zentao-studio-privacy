Privacy Policy — Rotation Control

Package: com.zentaostudio.rotation · Last updated: 2026-09-02

Rotation Control ("the app") is a screen-orientation utility. This policy is short because the app genuinely does nothing with your data.

Data collection

The app collects no data. No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no accounts, no crash-reporting SDKs, no network communication of any kind. The app does not contain internet permission.

What stays on your device

The app stores locally, on your device only:

  • Your selected rotation mode and on/off state
  • Your per-app rotation rules (a list of package names and chosen orientations)
  • Your previous auto-rotate setting, so it can be restored when you turn the app off

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or tap "Restore Android defaults".

Permissions

  • Display over other apps — used to hold the selected orientation with an invisible, zero-size window. The app draws nothing visible and cannot see your screen.
  • Modify system settings (optional) — used to write the system rotation setting so apps that follow the system orientation also rotate. The app reads and restores your previous auto-rotate value.
  • Usage access (optional) — used only when you create per-app rules, solely to learn which app is in the foreground so the matching rule can apply. The app never reads screen content, notifications, or any other usage information, and nothing is transmitted anywhere.
  • Run at startup (optional) — used to restore your rotation preference after a reboot, if you enable "Resume after restart".

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility, collects no data from anyone, and is suitable for all audiences.

Changes

Any future change to this policy will be published at this same location before it takes effect.

Contact
